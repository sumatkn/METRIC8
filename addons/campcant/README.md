# METRIC8: CampCant

Copy [METRIC8_CampCant](src/METRIC8_CampCant) into the Forever client's Interface/AddOns folder. Reload and type `/campcant`. The movable player opens at 316 × 104 UI units.

Click the song title to open the songbook. The second row switches racial cadence on or off. **Start** sings verse one in /say. **Next Verse** becomes available after the verse's reading time. The cue waits for your click. **Stop** ends the performance immediately and collapses the lyrics. You can also type `/campcant stop`. The player expands to fit the current verse while keeping its controls in place.

In the songbook, browse songs and verses with the arrows. Choose **Select song** to return to the player or **Rehearse privately** to run the timed verses without sending chat. **New song** starts a draft. Enter a title and the first verse, then use **Add verse** to insert another. Give each verse 2–120 seconds or leave timing blank for a word-count estimate. **Save song** stores it account-wide. **Edit a copy** lets you adapt a built-in. Each verse fits in one chat message (255 UTF-8 bytes).

The four built-ins include a campfire arrangement of the [Dark Iron Herald’s Brewfest chant](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Brewfest), an answer song quoting the opening of the [retired troll joke](https://thewowdb.com/sounds/troll-two-dwarves-joke/), and two original travel songs.
