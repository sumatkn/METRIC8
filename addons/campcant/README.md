# METRIC8: CampCant

Copy [METRIC8_CampCant](src/METRIC8_CampCant) into the Forever client's Interface/AddOns folder. Reload and type `/campcant`. The movable player opens at 316 × 104 UI units.

Click the song title to open the songbook. The second row switches racial cadence on or off. **Start** puts the first verse into the normal /say input. Press Enter to send it. **Next Verse** prepares the following verse on demand. An already-open chat entry stays yours until you send or close it. The last verse stays visible until you press **Stop**. **Stop** ends the performance immediately and collapses the lyrics. You can also type `/campcant stop`. The player expands to fit the current verse while keeping its controls in place.

In the songbook, browse songs and verses with the arrows. Choose **Select song** to return to the player or **Rehearse privately** to step through the verses without sending chat. **New song** starts a draft. Enter a title and the first verse, then use **Add verse** to insert another. **Save song** stores it account-wide. **Edit a copy** lets you adapt a built-in. Each verse fits in one chat message (255 UTF-8 bytes).

The historical entries contain short excerpts from the [Dark Iron Herald's Brewfest chant](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Brewfest) and the [original troll routine](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/forgotten-facts-about-wow/34502). Both are labeled as excerpts. The other two songs are CampCant originals. Use **Edit a copy** to add your own transcription.
